# Training Polish Audit Notes

## Browser review snapshot

- The training route already presents a credible course-player shell with strong dark theming, step framing, embedded deck visuals, and working lesson graphs.
- The top presentation-visuals panel currently reads more like a supporting gallery than the primary course canvas.
- The current lesson player has good structure, but the page navigator and lesson framing can still feel dense rather than premium.
- The contextual deck visual on the lesson page is useful, yet the overall experience would benefit from stronger narrative hierarchy, clearer section segmentation, and a more immersive slide-viewer feel.
- The graph cards are present and functional, but they still read as adjacent analytics blocks rather than tightly integrated evidence moments within the lesson story.
- The training page appears to emphasize text and cards more than a cohesive LMS-style visual progression.

## Highest-impact refinement directions

1. Make the lesson player itself feel more like the central course artifact, with stronger page framing, a clearer progress rail, and more premium sequencing cues.
2. Elevate deck-backed media into a larger, more deliberate presentation stage rather than a mostly static intro gallery.
3. Integrate the lesson charts into a stronger evidence strip or insight section so they feel like part of the instructional narrative.
4. Improve spacing, hierarchy, and navigation affordances so the training path feels less crowded and more editorial.
5. Add stronger module-family cues and evidence labels across the player so each content family feels intentionally mapped, not just displayed.

## Post-edit browser review

- The new top presentation block now reads much more like a dedicated course stage, with a larger learning canvas, filmstrip-like visual selectors, and embedded evidence context.
- The lesson-page header now communicates stage and section completion more clearly.
- The evidence graphics section is stronger because it introduces the charts as part of the lesson narrative instead of as generic analytics widgets.
- The page still has room for additional polish in the deeper stage surfaces and possibly the lower transfer/resource blocks, but the current direction is materially closer to a premium LMS-style product experience.
