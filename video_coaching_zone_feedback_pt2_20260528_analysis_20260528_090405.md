Based on the video provided, here is a summary of the product feedback, concrete edits requested, and prioritized recommendations.

### What is Working Well
*   **Overall Design:** The coaching section is highly praised, with the reviewer stating it looks "really, really good" and is very close to having a clean, "Apple" aesthetic.
*   **Functionality:** The core features of the weekly coaching log, such as the ability to add supporting documents and save the log, are working as intended.

### Concrete Edits Requested

**1. Layout & UI: Reduce Scrolling by Consolidating Content**
*   **Feedback:** The page is currently a bit too "scroll-heavy."
*   **Requested Edit:** The reviewer highlights the content in the right-hand column (which includes sections like "Coach's documentation feed," "Coach needs," and "Coach alerts") and requests that this information be moved and integrated into the tabbed navigation interface ("modes") on the left side of the screen (e.g., the tabs for Coaching Zone, Team roster, Documentation, Alerts).
*   **Goal:** By combining this content into the tabs, the layout will become a nearly single-page experience, drastically reducing the need for users to scroll.

**2. Workflow & UX: Add Privacy Options to Coaching Logs**
*   **Feedback:** Users need control over who is notified when a coaching log is created.
*   **Requested Edit:** Inside the "Weekly coaching log" modal, add an option to designate the coaching session as either "Public" or "Private."
    *   **Private:** The log is saved exclusively to the file, and no email notifications are sent to anyone.
    *   **Public:** The log is saved to the file and an email notification is automatically triggered.

### Prioritized Recommendations

**Priority 1: High Impact & Urgency - Restructure Page Layout**
*   **Action:** Move the right-column content into the left-side tabbed navigation.
*   **Reasoning:** This is the most significant UI/UX improvement requested. It directly addresses the primary friction point (excessive scrolling) and fundamentally improves how users will navigate and consume information on this page, making the interface much cleaner and more efficient.

**Priority 2: Medium Impact & Urgency - Implement Public/Private Toggle**
*   **Action:** Add the Public/Private selection to the "Weekly coaching log" modal with the corresponding email trigger logic.
*   **Reasoning:** This is a crucial workflow feature for managing communication and privacy. While important for the functionality of the tool, it is a localized, specific feature addition, whereas the layout change impacts the entire user experience of the page.
