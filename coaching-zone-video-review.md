## Practical Implementation Order

The safest implementation order is to start with the **visual readability fixes** first, because they are isolated and should not disturb working workflow logic. Next, remove or shrink the trend chart and reflow the **COACH NEEDS** section upward so the page becomes less scroll-heavy. After that, do a final pass on spacing and hierarchy so the documentation lane, alerts, and AI suggestion area feel like one cohesive coaching workflow rather than separate stacked blocks.

## Short Version

The video points to **four main edits**: make washed-out cards readable, make alerts more visible, remove the oversized trend chart, and move the COACH NEEDS/documentation section upward to reduce scrolling. Everything else in the coaching pop-up and documentation behavior should largely be preserved.
