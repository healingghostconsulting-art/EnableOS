# Neurosys Interactive Training Benchmark Notes

## Source Pages Reviewed

### 1. NeuroSYS homepage
- URL: https://neurosys.com/
- Relevant themes surfaced in the site copy include AI in education, augmented reality, recommender systems, audio processing, and personalized digital experiences.
- The homepage positions NeuroSYS as a builder of advanced applied systems rather than a simple content site, which implies that its strongest benchmark value for CHCG is likely in workflow depth, modality breadth, and enterprise-grade orchestration.

### 2. Learning management system (LMS) for Comcast
- URL: https://neurosys.com/case-studies/training-performance-compliance-platform
- NeuroSYS describes a platform for training, performance, compliance, and engagement serving approximately 40,000 users globally.
- The case study highlights two major user modules: administrators and learners.
- The functionality list explicitly includes a course catalogue, course builder, SCORM and Tin Can xAPI interoperability, instructor-led training and webinars, calendars, scheduling and reminders, quizzes, statistics, task assignment, notifications for mandatory learning, and progress tracking.
- The experience is framed not only as learning delivery, but as enterprise onboarding, staff training, compliance, engagement, and appraisal.
- A major differentiator is operational resilience: large assets are synchronized to local NAS infrastructure so learners can consume content smoothly even with weak connectivity.

## Early Benchmark Implications for CHCG EnableOS
- The target interaction level is broader than static course playback. It combines discovery, authoring, assignments, live training, scheduled follow-up, quizzes, reminders, learner progress, admin governance, and analytics in one connected system.
- The Comcast case suggests that enterprise learning products feel more advanced when they connect course consumption to scheduling, compliance, and managerial oversight rather than treating training as an isolated library.
- For CHCG specifically, this supports moving beyond narrated pages toward stronger role-based pathways, assessment states, live coaching checkpoints, and administrator-facing orchestration controls.

## Additional Neurosys Findings

### 3. Augmented Reality services
- URL: https://neurosys.com/services/augmented-reality
- NeuroSYS explicitly frames advanced training as guided, in-context, hands-free work support in the real environment rather than passive media consumption.
- Their page highlights a drag-and-drop AR training editor, real-time step guidance, and remote expert assistance where consultants can see the technician’s field of view and provide both verbal and visual instruction.
- The interaction pattern here is procedural and contextual: learners are guided through exactly what to do, in sequence, with environmental relevance and expert escalation available during execution.

### 4. AI in Education & Training
- URL: https://neurosys.com/services/ai-in-education
- NeuroSYS emphasizes dissatisfaction with traditional lecture-style learning and argues for stimulation, autonomy, personalization, and adaptive pacing.
- The service framing highlights multi-format course delivery, AI-powered learning management, AR training, autonomous content generation, recommender systems, individualized learning paths, automated assessment and grading, immediate feedback, security controls, and analytics.
- The dominant pattern is not a single flashy interface element. It is a coordinated system that personalizes what learners see, adapts what comes next, measures progress continuously, and gives managers operational visibility.

## Updated Benchmark Readout for CHCG
- The Neurosys benchmark suggests that “interactive” should mean guided action, adaptive branching, immediate assessment feedback, role-based pathways, managerial orchestration, and contextual support instead of just better visuals or narration.
- The strongest analogs for CHCG are searchable discovery, role-aligned learning paths, embedded checkpoints, live or simulated coaching, evidence capture, administrator controls, reminders, progress analytics, and real workflow context carried into the training surface.
- If CHCG wants to match this standard credibly, each training should feel like a working system: it should orient the learner, simulate decisions, check comprehension, adapt the next step, and connect outcomes back to coaching and governance.
