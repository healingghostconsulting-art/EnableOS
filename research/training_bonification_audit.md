# Three Agent Training Bonification Audit

## Scope

This audit focuses on the first three agent trainings currently mapped in the product: **active listening**, **confident reassurance without overpromising**, and **de-escalation and professional recovery**. The purpose is not to reframe them around healthcare research at this stage, but to identify how to make the trainings themselves feel more complete, better crafted, and more effective inside the CHCG learning experience.

## What is already working

The current modules already have a solid instructional skeleton. Each training has a clear hero, multiple core lesson slides, practice slides, an apply checkpoint, coach prompts, reflection prompts, a scenario, and a gated application activity. That means the foundational structure is strong enough to improve without rebuilding the whole learning model.

The writing also already aims for observable behaviors rather than vague inspiration. The modules consistently emphasize ownership, next-step clarity, and coachable language. That gives the next revision a strong base.

## Highest-impact improvement opportunities

| Module | Current strengths | Main gaps to fix | Bonification direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active listening | Strong opening pattern, clear CHCG listening model, coachable checkpoint logic | Slides feel slightly repetitive in structure; the modeled language is useful but could sound more vivid and less template-like; the module could use stronger contrast between weak and strong openings | Make the listening lesson feel more cinematic and diagnostic by showing the emotional cue, the agent’s internal decision, and the visible language shift from friction to control |
| Reassurance | Clear distinction between confidence and overpromising; good audit-ready framing; practical phrase bank direction | Some phrasing is still abstract; the module needs more nuanced examples of how confidence sounds under pressure; visual storytelling can be more distinctive than contrast boards alone | Turn reassurance into a sharper “language under uncertainty” module with stronger side-by-side rewrites, better moment-by-moment scripts, and more memorable quote-worthy coaching lines |
| De-escalation | Strong opening-middle-close structure; clear control-recovery sequence; good application prompts | The recovery flow could feel more immersive; some lines are functional but not yet vivid; the lesson can do more to show how tone, pacing, and sequencing change the interaction | Make the de-escalation module feel like a guided recovery storyboard, with clearer emotional progression, stronger recovery phrases, and more concrete “what the customer hears” framing |

## Cross-module patterns that should improve

Across all three modules, the lesson architecture is consistent, but the experiences still feel a little too evenly structured. To feel more intentionally crafted, each module should have a more distinct identity.

The **active listening** training should feel like pattern recognition and controlled opening. The **reassurance** training should feel like precision language under uncertainty. The **de-escalation** training should feel like emotional recovery and professional command under pressure.

A second improvement area is the relationship between slide text and narration. Right now, the content is solid, but much of it reads like concise instructional copy. The next pass should let the narration do more of the coaching and interpretation, while the slide text becomes more visual, sharper, and easier to scan.

A third improvement area is the use of supporting statements. The strongest modules usually contain one or two lines that sound memorable enough for a learner to carry into practice. These CHCG trainings should gain more of those crafted lines — not slogans, but short, practical phrases that feel like something a strong coach would actually say.

## Recommended creative direction

The next content pass should treat each module as a **guided performance lesson** rather than a deck translation. That means each lesson page should do one of four things very clearly: frame the moment, contrast a weak and strong behavior, model usable language, or coach the learner on what to listen for in themselves.

The content should move away from merely describing good communication and toward **showing the turning point** inside the conversation. In practical terms, this means stronger modeled phrases, clearer before-and-after wording, sharper observational cues, and better narration notes that explain why the moment matters.

## Specific rewrite targets

| Content surface | Upgrade target |
| --- | --- |
| Hero summary | Make it more human and outcome-oriented, with less “module description” tone |
| Core slides | Increase distinctiveness so each page has a unique job instead of following nearly identical wording patterns |
| Practice slides | Add richer realism and more vivid learner decision moments |
| Apply step | Make the stakes feel slightly more concrete, while keeping the current gating structure |
| Coach prompts | Make them sound more like actual coaching questions used after a monitored interaction |
| Reflection prompts | Replace generic self-reflection with stronger action commitments the learner could use on the next call |
| Narration guidance | Add speaker-note style coaching language that interprets the lesson instead of repeating the slide |

## Implementation direction

The next implementation pass should focus on improving copy quality, adding stronger supporting statements, making visuals feel more intentional per module, and enriching narration notes so the trainings feel less templated and more authored.
