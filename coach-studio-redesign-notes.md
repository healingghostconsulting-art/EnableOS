# Coach Studio Redesign Notes

## Current live-preview findings

The current Coach Studio screen still shows the exact issues raised in the feedback video. The upper-right hero support cards appear low-contrast and washed out, especially the learner-focus card and the surrounding supporting surfaces. The layout still places a large coach-visible trend chart directly above the supervision lane, which consumes substantial vertical space and pushes the documentation-oriented workflow further down the page.

The page structure remains scroll-heavy because the hero, chart section, supervision lane, and tabbed work areas are stacked vertically. The AI coaching suggestion and weekly coaching-log pop-up flow appear intact and should be preserved during the redesign. The reviewer-approved direction is to make the screen feel tighter, calmer, more premium, and more directly action-oriented by replacing noisy panels with cleaner side-channel summary surfaces and better contrast.
