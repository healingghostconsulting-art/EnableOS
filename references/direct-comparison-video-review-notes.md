## Current interface alignment notes for mockup

The current published homepage already uses the right overall brand language and visual tone: a premium neutral canvas, dark editorial typography, soft cards, and muted gold/teal accents. However, the hero still dedicates a very large amount of vertical space to narrative framing before users reach denser mission browsing.

When attempting to enter the interactive simulator from the public homepage, the flow immediately routes through sign-in. That confirms the mockup should focus on the public browse-and-launch shell and the authenticated mission or training-entry pattern rather than assuming a public deep player. The redesign concept should therefore compact the homepage and mission-entry pattern without losing the current premium tone.
