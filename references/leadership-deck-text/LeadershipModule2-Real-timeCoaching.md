## Slide 7

The Benefits of Setting SMART Goals
SMART GOALS
SMART Goals are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound goals that help you focus on what you want to achieve.
BENEFITS
SMART Goals provide clarity and focus, help you track progress, and increase motivation.
EXAMPLE
For example, a SMART Goal could be to increase website traffic by 10% in the next 3 months. By modifying the current marketing strategy.
SMART GOALS ARE AN EFFECTIVE WAY TO SET AND ACHIEVE YOUR OBJECTIVES. BY SETTING SMART GOALS, YOU CAN FOCUS ON WHAT YOU WANT TO ACHIEVE AND TRACK YOUR PROGRESS ALONG THE WAY.

## Slide 13

Smart Goal: Real-time Coaching
SPECIFIC Goals should have clear outcomes, not vague or general ideas. MEASURABLE Goals should have quantitative targets to track progress. ACHIEVABLE Goals should push limits but still be realistic given resources and capabilities.
RELEVANT Goals should align with overall objectives and focus on critical activities. TIME-BOUND Goals should have a defined time frame to create urgency and prompt action.

## Slide 14

How to Write a SMART Goal Activity
DEFINE YOUR GOAL Clearly state what you want to accomplish in specific, measurable terms. MAKE IT ACHIEVABLE Ensure your goal is realistic and attainable within the given timeframe. INCLUDE A TIMEFRAME Provide a specific deadline for achieving the goal.
LIST ACTIONS TO ACHIEVE IT Outline the specific steps you will take to accomplish the goal. IDENTIFY RESOURCES NEEDED Determine any tools, skills, or support needed to complete each action step.

Exploring Different Learning Styles
VISUAL LEARNING
Learning through seeing, such as diagrams, charts, and videos
AUDITORY LEARNING

Identifying an Agent's Learning Style

Why Owning the Message as a Leader is Critical
As a leader, it is important to own the message even when you are not in agreement with the decision. This shows your team that you are committed to the decision and will support them in carrying it out.
